# Support OpenYAMM Atlas

OpenYAMM Atlas is a free, open-source community project maintained by **Karol**, building a native interactive
Atlas for Might & Magic VII and VIII on top of [OpenYAMM](https://github.com/pjasicek/openyamm).

## Why support helps

I currently work with **ChatGPT Plus**. Its subscription usage limits strongly constrain how much development
I can do. I have more work I could take on, but the available capacity does not let me keep pace with the
OpenYAMM author's frequent updates.

If community support allows me to upgrade to a higher-usage plan, I will use that extra capacity to
**substantially accelerate Atlas development**, spend more time on compatibility and testing, and make
sustained collaboration with the OpenYAMM author more practical.

The initial funding goal is development capacity: higher-usage AI tools for the Atlas and its upstream port.
Progress and changes to that goal will be reported in the repository. Support helps fund this work; it does
not purchase game assets, an official OpenYAMM release, a feature deadline or a guaranteed integration outcome.
The source remains freely available under its existing license.

## Other ways to help

- Report reproducible issues, with the source revision, world/map and steps to reproduce.
- Review the [Atlas integration handover](https://github.com/coral18/openyamm/blob/extended/atlas-mm8/ATLAS_HANDOFF.md) or contribute a focused compatibility fix.
- Share the project with Might & Magic players interested in an in-game Atlas.
