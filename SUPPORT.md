# Support OpenYAMM Atlas

I'm Karol, and I'm building a native interactive Atlas for Might & Magic VII and VIII on top of
[OpenYAMM](https://github.com/pjasicek/openyamm). OpenYAMM Atlas is a free, open-source community project.

## Why support helps

I use ChatGPT Plus and regularly hit its usage limits. There is more work I'd like to do, but those limits
slow me down and prevent me from keeping up with the OpenYAMM author's frequent updates.

If support lets me upgrade to a plan with higher limits, I'll use it to get much more development and
testing done. That means faster progress on the Atlas and its compatibility with newer OpenYAMM releases.
It would also make collaboration with the author more practical. I'd like to use that capacity for other
Might & Magic mods and tools, and try out more ideas for the community.

One day, I'd love to build a larger original RPG in Unreal Engine that captures the spirit of Might & Magic.
I haven't started that game; it's something I'd like to work towards.

Contributions help pay for AI tools with higher usage limits across my projects, starting with the Atlas
and its upstream port. I'll share progress and new projects as I work on them. OpenYAMM Atlas remains free
and open source under its existing license.

## Other ways to help

- Report reproducible issues, with the source revision, world/map and steps to reproduce.
- Review the [Atlas integration handover](https://github.com/coral18/openyamm/blob/extended/atlas-mm8/ATLAS_HANDOFF.md) or contribute a focused compatibility fix.
- Share the project with Might & Magic players interested in an in-game Atlas.
